📊 CTC Breakdown — What Each Component Means
How to read and interpret this chart
Basic (40% of CTC)
Foundation. PF, Gratuity, LOP all calculated on Basic. Higher Basic = more retirement savings.
HRA (20% of CTC)
Tax-exempt if you pay rent. Exempt = min(HRA received, 50%/40% Basic, Actual Rent – 10% Basic).
Special Allowance
Fully taxable filler component. Can be restructured via FBP to reduce tax.
PF Employer (12% Basic)
Company's PF contribution. Goes to your EPFO account. 8.33% to EPS pension, rest to EPF.
Other Allowances
LTA, food, telephone, transport — partially tax-exempt per Income Tax Act limits.
📊 Perquisite Tax u/s 17(2) — Ranges
How to read and interpret this chart
No Perquisites
Zero additional tax. Simplest salary structure.
Car ≤1600cc
₹1,800/mo taxable value (₹21,600/year added to income).
Car >1600cc
₹2,400/mo taxable (₹28,800/year). Plus ₹900/mo if driver provided.
Accommodation (Metro)
15% of Basic salary as annual taxable perquisite value.
Interest-Free Loan
Taxable at SBI rate (7.45% FY2026) on outstanding principal.
