Privacy notice

Workforce records

The application stores account and employment details and the records entered in modules such as attendance, payroll, benefits, and case management. Your role and relationship to a record determine which information you can access.

Sign-in and device information

Sign-in records can include the date and time, device and browser details, and IP address. For passkeys, your device verifies your identity and sends a signed response. Workforce Intelligence stores credential information used to verify that response. Fingerprint and face scans stay on your device.

Location and weather

Location preferences are available in Application Settings. IP lookup sends your public IP address to IPWho.is when selected. Browser-based location requires permission. Coordinates supplied to the application may be sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service for place names and Open-Meteo for current weather.

AI chat and document excerpts

When chat is enabled, the selected AI provider receives the messages you submit. If you include workforce context or select a document to use as context, the provider receives the applicable totals or retrieved excerpts. Avoid sharing confidential or personal information unless your organisation has approved the provider and use case. Check with your administrator for the active provider and applicable terms.

Browser storage

The app stores your session token and display preferences in your browser. A deployment with the demo gate enabled also uses an access cookie. See the browser storage notice for details.

Retention and contact

Contact your organisation’s workforce administrator about its retention policy, access or correction requests, and privacy questions. If you have a workforce account, you can contact your administrator through the helpdesk.
