Browser storage notice

Sign-in

The app uses browser storage for the signed-in session and account display information. Sign out from the Account menu to clear those values and end the session.

Demo access

When the demo gate is enabled, the wfi_demo_access cookie records successful entry for up to seven days.

Preferences

Browser storage remembers preferences such as the theme. Clearing site data in your browser also clears these preferences and may require you to sign in again.
