Security and privacy

This demo stores workforce records in MySQL. It does not certify regulatory compliance.

Account protection

Passwords are hashed. Enrolled passkeys use signed device challenges; biometric information stays on your device. Login and attendance record a server-observed IP address. IP addresses do not prove physical presence.

Private data

AI provider keys and bank account numbers are encrypted with keys in the persistent private state volume. Back up that volume with the database. Location and weather services are optional account settings.

Report a concern

Use the helpdesk to create a tracked request. This demo does not notify an external security team or provide an emergency hotline.
