📊 OKR Progress — Range Interpretation
How to read and interpret this chart
0–30% — Off Track (Red)
Significantly behind schedule. Risk of missing quarterly objective. Escalate blockers. Consider scope reduction.
31–60% — At Risk (Amber)
Behind expected pace. Mid-quarter review needed. Identify root causes and resource constraints.
61–85% — On Track (Green)
Good progress. Expected to achieve target by end of quarter. Continue current pace.
86–100% — Achieved (Blue)
Target met or exceeded. Document learnings. Feed into next quarter's stretch goals.
>100% — Stretch Achieved
Exceeded ambitious target. Signals underestimation OR exceptional performance. Recalibrate goal difficulty next cycle.
📊 OKR Grading Philosophy
How to read and interpret this chart
Score 0.7 (70%) = Perfect OKR
Google/Intel standard: if you're scoring 100% every time, goals aren't ambitious enough. 70% means you set real stretch goals.
Score <0.4 = Re-examine
Below 40% indicates either the goal was too ambitious OR something fundamentally blocked execution. Address root cause.
Score 0.4–0.6 = Review & Adjust
Acceptable but requires mid-cycle review. Reforecast if external factors changed significantly.
Score 0.7–1.0 = Ideal Range
Hit your OKRs consistently in this band. Demonstrates ambitious goal-setting with realistic execution.
