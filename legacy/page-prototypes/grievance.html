📊 Grievance Escalation SLA — Response Times
How to read and interpret this chart
L1 — Manager (3 days)
First point of contact. Most issues resolved here. Informal discussion + documented resolution.
L2 — HR Manager (7 days)
Unresolved or sensitive issues. Formal written complaint. HR mediates between employee and manager.
L3 — HR Head (15 days)
Complex cases: harassment, policy violation, discrimination. Investigation committee formed.
L4 — MD/Legal (30 days)
Final internal escalation. External arbitration possible. Legal team may be involved.
Labour Court (ID Act 1947)
Legal remedy if all internal channels exhausted. Conciliation officer → Labour Court → High Court.
📊 Grievance Priority Classification
How to read and interpret this chart
Low Priority
Administrative issues: leave denial, shift change, minor policy clarifications. Resolve at L1.
Medium Priority
Pay discrepancy, performance rating dispute, work allocation. L2 within 7 days.
High Priority
Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety concerns. L3 immediate. POSH committee if sexual harassment.
Critical
Whistleblower complaint, fraud, legal violation. L4 + Legal within 24 hours. Full investigation.
