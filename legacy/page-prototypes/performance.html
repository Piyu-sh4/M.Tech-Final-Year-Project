📊 Performance Rating Distribution Explained
How to read and interpret this chart
Below Expectations (1–1.9)
Employee needs immediate improvement. PIP is initiated. Salary revision withheld. Managed Exit may apply if no improvement in 90 days.
Partially Meets Expectations (2–2.9)
Meets some but not all KPIs. Coaching plan assigned. Eligible for partial increment (0–3%). Performance monitored quarterly.
Meets Expectations (3–3.9)
Solid performer. Eligible for standard increment (5–8%). Core Player in 9-Box. No PIP required.
Exceeds Expectations (4–4.4)
Top performer. Eligible for accelerated increment (10–15%). Promotion consideration. High Achiever / Future Star in 9-Box.
Outstanding (4.5–5.0)
Star HiPo. Fast-track promotion eligible. ESOPs / spot awards / leadership program nominations. Top 5% of workforce.
📊 9-Box Talent Grid — Reading Guide
How to read and interpret this chart
Manage Out (Low Pot / Low Perf)
Immediate PIP. Exit counselling if no improvement in 60 days. Typically 2–5% of workforce.
Develop / Inconsistent (Medium range)
Coaching, mentoring, and training plan. Watch list. Needs consistent performance over 2 quarters to move up.
Core Player (Medium Pot / Medium Perf)
Backbone of the organisation — 50–60% of workforce. Standard increment and stable career growth.
High Achiever / Trusted Pro (High Perf)
Stretch assignments, leadership exposure. 10–18% increment. Succession pipeline candidates.
Star HiPo (High Pot / High Perf)
Maximum increment, ESOP, leadership fast-track. Global exposure opportunities. Top 5% priority retention.
