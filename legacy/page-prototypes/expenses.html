📊 Leave Balance — Range Guide
How to read and interpret this chart
High Balance (>60% remaining)
Plenty of leave available. Plan leaves to avoid lapsing at year-end. March 31st carry-forward limit applies.
Normal Balance (40–60%)
On track. Balanced usage. No action needed.
Low Balance (20–39%)
Use cautiously. Additional leaves may result in LOP.
Critical (<20%)
Any absence = Loss of Pay. LOP = Basic ÷ 26 × days.
Zero / LOP Applied
All absences deducted from salary. Impacts increment eligibility.
📊 LOP Formula — Exact Calculation
How to read and interpret this chart
Formula
LOP = Basic Salary ÷ 26 working days × Number of LOP days
Example
Basic ₹44,000 × 2 days LOP = ₹44,000 ÷ 26 × 2 = ₹3,385 deducted
3+ LOP days
Appears on payslip. Impacts credit score. Considered in annual review.
LOP vs Unpaid
LOP = zero balance + absent. Long-term LOP needs HR approval.
