📊 Analytics Dashboard — Chart Interpretation
How to read and interpret this chart
Revenue Trend (Green line)
Month-on-month revenue. Upward slope = healthy growth. Flat = plateau. Investigate if declining for 2+ months.
Headcount Bar Chart
Blue bars = actual headcount per dept. Compare to target line. Gap = open positions to fill urgently.
Cost Per Hire (Amber)
Ideal range: ₹25,000–₹80,000. Higher = sourcing process inefficiency. Lower = possible quality compromise.
Attrition Rate (Red)
Industry benchmark 8–12%. Above 15% = critical. Drill down by dept, tenure, and reason code.
Productivity Index (Purple)
Revenue per employee. Rising = efficiency gain. Declining = overhead growing faster than output.
📊 KPI Health Indicators
How to read and interpret this chart
Green KPI
On target or above. Continue current strategy. Share with leadership as win.
Amber KPI
Within 10% of target. Action plan needed within 2 weeks. Monitor weekly.
Red KPI
Missed target by >10%. Root cause analysis + recovery plan. Escalate to C-suite.
