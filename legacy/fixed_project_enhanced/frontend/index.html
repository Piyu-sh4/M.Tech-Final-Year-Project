About This Project

Built to solve real
HR challenges

The Workforce Intelligence Platform is a full-stack enterprise HR system developed as a 4th Semester B.Tech project at IIT Patna. It replicates the complete workflow of a modern IT company — from onboarding to payroll, attendance tracking to AI-driven attrition prediction.

🤖
AI-Powered Attrition Prediction Python ML model predicts employee churn risk using attendance, task, salary and behaviour signals
🔐
JWT Role-Based Security Admin, Manager, HR and Employee roles with granular feature-level permission control
⚡
Real-Time WebSocket Alerts Socket.io push notifications for overdue tasks, leave approvals and shift changes
📄
PDF Payslip Generation Downloadable monthly salary slips with itemized breakdown: base, bonus, deductions, net
Node.js + Express MySQL Socket.io Python Flask Claude AI JWT Auth RandomForest ML Redis Cache
Platform Features

Everything HR needs,
nothing it doesn't

Eight fully functional modules that cover the entire employee lifecycle from hire to retire.

💰
Payroll & Salary Management
Itemized payslips with base salary, bonus, PF, ESI, TDS deductions. Bulk salary generation and mark-as-paid workflow. Downloadable PDF payslips.
📅
Attendance & Shift Tracking
Clock-in / clock-out with live status. Admin attendance marking, overtime calculation, monthly summaries and shift scheduling.
🏖️
Leave Management
Annual, sick, casual and optional leave types. Balance tracking, manager approvals, half-day requests and company holiday calendar.
📋
Task Intelligence
Create, assign and track tasks with priority levels, countdown timers, file attachments and extension request workflow.
🤖
AI Chat Assistant
Claude AI-powered chat for HR queries, policy lookup and analytics insights. Role-aware responses for each user level.
📊
Attrition Prediction
RandomForest ML model scores each employee's flight risk using 8 behavioural signals. Risk breakdown with explainable factors.
🏆
Leaderboard & Gamification
Points system rewards task completion. Achievement badges unlock higher access levels. Keeps teams motivated and competitive.
🔍
Immutable Audit Log
Every admin action is permanently logged with timestamp, actor, IP and change detail. Tamper-proof compliance trail.
Company Guidelines

IT Company Policy
at a Glance

📋
WorkforceIQ Technology Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Policy Handbook
Effective from April 1, 2025 · Version 3.2 · HR Department
⏰
Working Hours
  • Standard hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Mon–Fri)
  • Flexible timing window: 8:30 AM – 10:00 AM start
  • Minimum 8 hours/day logged in attendance system
  • Overtime (>9h) eligible for comp-off or OT pay
  • Work from Home: up to 2 days/week with manager approval
  • Saturday: alternate Saturdays working (9 AM – 1 PM)
🏖️
Leave Policy
  • Annual Leave (AL): 18 days per calendar year
  • Sick Leave (SL): 12 days — no carry-forward
  • Casual Leave (CL): 6 days — max 3 consecutive
  • Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (as per law)
  • Paternity Leave: 5 days
  • Leave application minimum 2 days in advance
  • Unused AL (up to 10 days) carries forward to next year
💻
IT & Device Policy
  • Company laptop for official use only
  • No personal software installation without IT approval
  • VPN mandatory when accessing internal systems remotely
  • All data stored on approved cloud (Google Drive / SharePoint)
  • USB drives prohibited on office premises
  • Device must be locked when leaving desk
  • Quarterly mandatory security awareness training
🔒
Data & Confidentiality
  • NDA signed at onboarding — valid 2 years post-exit
  • Client data classified as Strictly Confidential
  • No client/project info shared on personal social media
  • Source code cannot be stored outside company repositories
  • Password policy: min 12 chars, change every 90 days
  • Report security incidents within 1 hour of discovery
💰
Salary & Benefits
  • Salary credited on or before 5th of every month
  • Annual increment cycle: April (performance-linked)
  • Performance bonus: up to 15% of annual CTC
  • PF: 12% employee + 12% employer contribution
  • Health insurance: ₹5 Lakh cover (employee + spouse + 2 kids)
  • Internet reimbursement: ₹1,000/month for WFH
⚠️
Code of Conduct
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying
  • Dress code: smart casuals on weekdays; casuals on Friday
  • Mobile phones on silent in meeting rooms
  • 3 tardiness warnings trigger formal HR review
  • Moonlighting prohibited without written HR approval
  • Notice period: 30 days (junior) / 60 days (senior)
📈
Training & Growth
  • Annual learning budget: ₹20,000 per employee
  • Approved certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure) — 100% reimbursed
  • Internal tech talks every Friday 4–5 PM
  • Mandatory 40 hours of L&D activity per year
  • Mentorship programme for new joiners (first 90 days)
  • Promotion reviewed bi-annually (April & October)

Health & Wellbeing
